# ✍️ Draw Your Font: Typography & Vector Glyph Engine

Draw Your Font empowers agents to generate, inspect, and optimize custom vector typefaces and bespoke typography assets.

---

## 🎯 Capabilities
1. **Vector Glyph Generation**: Generates mathematically sound SVG paths for Latin, digits, and punctuation glyphs.
2. **Metrics & Kerning Calculations**: Configures baseline, x-height, cap-height, ascenders, descenders, and pair-kerning tables.
3. **Web Font Compilation**: Formats SVG glyph sets for compilation into WOFF2 / TTF font packages.
4. **Display Lettering**: Crafts bespoke SVG titles and brand logotypes with custom bezier contours.
