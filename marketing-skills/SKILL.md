# 📈 Marketing Skills: Growth, Copywriting & Positioning Playbooks

This skill equips agents with proven marketing, conversion, and growth frameworks for developers, founders, and product teams.

---

## 🎯 Core Frameworks

### 1. Landing Page Copy Architectures
- **Problem-Agitate-Solve (PAS)**:
  - Identify the burning pain point.
  - Show the cost/friction of leaving it unsolved.
  - Present the product as the obvious, friction-free remedy.
- **Attention-Interest-Desire-Action (AIDA)**:
  - Strong specific headline that breaks pattern.
  - Proof points and architecture details that build genuine interest.
  - Social proof and case studies driving desire.
  - Single, unmistakable Call to Action (CTA).

### 2. Value Proposition Formula
Every great positioning statement answers three questions in 15 words or fewer:
1. What does it do?
2. Who is it specifically for?
3. What makes it dramatically better or different than the incumbent alternative?

### 3. High-Converting Page Audit Checklist
- Above the fold: Headline, subhead, primary CTA, and concrete visual preview of the real product.
- Zero generic placeholder icons: Show actual code, dashboards, or workflows.
- Social proof: Customer quotes with specific metrics, company logos.
- Overcome objections: FAQs addressing security, pricing, migration, and integration questions.
