# ✍️ Humanizer: Natural Human Voice Engine

The Humanizer skill strips detectable AI tropes and corporate fluff, ensuring all written prose sounds like it was written by an observant, thoughtful human engineer or writer.

---

## 🚫 Banned AI Slop & Buzzwords

Never use:
- **Verbs**: Delve, embark, foster, garner, bolster, showcase, underscore, champion, harness, unleash.
- **Nouns**: Tapestry, testament, beacon, realm, paradigm, landscape, synergy, powerhouse.
- **Adjectives**: Pivotal, crucial, robust, vibrant, meticulous, seamless, cutting-edge, state-of-the-art, game-changing.
- **Formulas**: "Not only... but also", "It's important to remember", "In conclusion", forced groupings of three adjectives.

---

## ✅ The Human Voice Rules

1. **Write with Simple Verbs**: Use "make", "build", "run", "fix", "find", "use", "show" instead of inflated corporate synonyms.
2. **Be Specific**: Give exact numbers, file names, people, and measurements rather than abstract praise.
3. **Vary Sentence Length**: Mix short punchy observations with natural, compound sentences.
4. **State Facts Plainly**: If a feature works, say what it does. Don't say it "revolutionizes the developer workflow".
5. **Cut Conclusions**: End when the information ends. Never write a paragraph summarizing what you just said.
